# Client launch advertisement concept: Scholar Studio

## Creative direction
A 35-45 second product-led launch spot for prospective education and training clients. Core promise: turn the documents a learner already has into cited answers, usable quizzes, and a study plan. The visual anchor should be authentic screen capture of the running application; restrained kinetic typography and source-citation callouts support it. No music. Optional warm, clear voiceover, with captions for accessibility.

## Suggested beat structure
1. 0-5s: Problem hook. A dense stack of course materials; text: "Your learning material is everywhere. Your understanding should not be."
2. 5-12s: Product reveal. Capture Scholar Studio opening to the three-panel workspace; sources appear in the left pane.
3. 12-20s: Grounded chat. Ask a question; show a concise answer and click a citation to reveal the source excerpt/page.
4. 20-29s: Active recall. Generate a quiz from the same sources, answer one question, reveal its cited explanation.
5. 29-36s: Study support. Show study-guide synthesis and mastery analytics. Do not imply podcast/audio overview exists until implemented and tested.
6. 36-42s: CTA. "Bring your materials. Learn with evidence." Scholar Studio wordmark and a client-approved call to action.

## Voiceover draft
"Your course materials are full of answers. Finding them, and knowing what to remember, takes time. Scholar Studio turns your PDFs and notes into a learning workspace grounded in your own sources. Ask a question, follow its citation back to the page, then test what you understand with a quiz built from the same material. Review key concepts with a study guide and see where to focus next. Scholar Studio. Learn from your material, with evidence."

## Production constraints
- No music; voiceover optional. Use natural pauses and subtle interface sounds only if approved.
- Record the real app; avoid synthetic UI or claims not verified in the running build.
- Blur/redact personal, client, or local system data before rendering.
- Do not advertise podcast generation yet: no podcast UI or API has been found in the current application.
- Runtime must be selected with the client/user between OpenMontage-supported Remotion and HyperFrames before a production render.
